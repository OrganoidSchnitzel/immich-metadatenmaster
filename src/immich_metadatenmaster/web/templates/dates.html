Date photos

  1. Put a batch of photos (e.g. scans) into its own Immich album and let Immich finish face detection.
  2. Name the people in Immich and set their birthdays.
  3. Open the album here. Write the date you know, estimate it from the ages of the people in the photos, or take it from the file names. Suggestions are only written when you accept them.

Photos without a date that are not in an album: the library overview on the start page lists them by month and opens them like an album.

Versions with a different date

HDR merges, panoramas and edited copies (IMG_1234-HDR.dng, IMG_1234_edited.jpg, IMG_1234 (1).jpg …) often lose the capture date, so Immich shows them on the day they were exported. This finds them and suggests the date of the original (IMG_1234.CR2), and its location if the version has none.
