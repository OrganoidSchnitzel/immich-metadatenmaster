Check & fix

Finds mistakes in the dates and locations already in Immich, and suggests a fix for each.

What to check

and/or this time range (optional)
Advanced
Nothing is changed until you click Fix.
What is checked?
  • Camera clocks. Each camera's photos are compared with your phone's photos of the same moments (by time, and by looks with Immich's smart search). Phones set their clocks themselves. A camera that was off gets its times corrected.
  • Locations that don't fit the GPX track at the photo's (corrected) time, e.g. written earlier with a wrong camera clock. Your phone's own GPS positions are left alone.
  • Locations placed by hand that are far from your phone's photos of the same minutes, e.g. a whole day put on one spot on the map.
  • Home time instead of local time: photos from a camera that stores no time zone are shown in its home time (e.g. German time in Colorado). Setting the local time of the place fixes that.
  • Photos without a location that fit a GPX track once their camera's clock is corrected.

Every fix is logged in the History and can be undone there.
